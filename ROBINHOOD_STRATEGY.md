## 🎯 **STRATEGY RULES**

### **Entry Conditions (ALL must be met):**

1. **Strong Trend Required**
   - ADX > 25 (Ensures strong directional movement)
   
2. **EMA Crossover**
   - Long: 13 EMA crosses above 34 EMA
   - Short: 13 EMA crosses below 34 EMA

3. **Volume Confirmation**
   - Relative Volume > 1.2x average (High participation)

### **Position Sizing:**
- Risk 2% of capital per trade
- Position sized based on ATR stop loss

### **Stop Loss:**
- 1.0 × ATR from entry price
- Tight stops = Quick exits if wrong

### **Take Profit:**
- Target 1: 3.0 × ATR (Primary target)
- Target 2: 4.5 × ATR (Extended target)
- Target 3: 6.0 × ATR (Home run target)

### **Exit Strategy:**
- Scale out: 50% at TP1, 25% at TP2, 25% at TP3
- Stop loss always active
- No holding overnight positions (optional, adjust for risk tolerance)

## 🤖 **ROBINHOOD EXECUTION GUIDE**

### **Tools You Need:**
1. **Robinhood Account** (Obviously)
2. **TradingView or Similar** (For indicators - Free tier works)
3. **Alerts** (Set up notifications for setups)

### **Step-by-Step:**

#### **1. Set Up Your Chart**
- Symbol: SPY
- Timeframe: 1 Hour
- Add Indicators:
  - EMA(13) - Green line
  - EMA(34) - Red line
  - ADX(14) - Separate panel
  - Volume with 20-bar moving average

#### **2. Scan for Setups (Daily)**
Check at market close (4:00 PM ET):
- Is ADX > 25? (Strong trend)
- Is 13 EMA close to 34 EMA? (Potential crossover)
- Is volume > 1.2x average? (High participation)

#### **3. Place Alert**
If conditions are brewing, set alert:
- "SPY 13 EMA crosses above/below 34 EMA"
- Check ADX and volume when alert triggers

#### **4. Entry Execution**
When all conditions met:
1. Calculate position size: Risk $200 (2% of $10k)
2. Calculate stop loss: Entry price ± (1.0 × ATR)
3. Calculate position: $200 ÷ (Stop distance) = Shares
4. Place order on Robinhood:
   - Market or limit order
   - Set stop loss immediately
   - Set take profit orders (if available) or manual exit

#### **5. Management**
- Let the trade work
- Don't micromanage
- Exit 50% at +3 ATR
- Trail stop on remaining 50%
