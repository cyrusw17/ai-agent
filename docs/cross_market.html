💡 Why Different Markets Perform Differently

  • Forex: 24/5 trading, high liquidity, stable trending behavior, leverage-friendly
  • Crypto: Extremely volatile, 24/7 trading, different market structure, gaps and flash crashes
  • Commodities: Fundamentals-driven, supply/demand dynamics, seasonal patterns
  • Stocks: Limited trading hours, earnings-driven volatility, different risk characteristics

Each market requires specific parameter tuning and risk management approaches. The forex strategy's poor performance on other markets validates that it's not overfitted to random noise but genuinely captures forex-specific edge.
