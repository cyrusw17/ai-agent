## 📋 **IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE**

### **Step 1: Setup**
1. Start with $10,000 minimum
2. Choose broker with 1:50+ leverage
3. Focus on 4 major pairs: AUDUSD, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY
4. Use daily timeframe

### **Step 2: Position Sizing**
```
Current DD < 3%:  Risk 4% per trade, max 10x leverage
Current DD 3-6%:  Risk 2.5% per trade, max 8x leverage
Current DD 6-8%:  Risk 1.5% per trade, max 5x leverage
Current DD > 8%:  Risk 1% per trade, max 3x leverage
```

### **Step 3: Trade Execution**
1. Wait for EMA 5/13 crossover
2. Confirm ADX > 15
3. Enter at market
4. Stop: 1.5 × ATR
5. Target: 4.0 × ATR

### **Step 4: Money Management**
1. Track equity daily
2. Calculate current drawdown from peak
3. Every Friday: Withdraw profits > $10k
4. Reset peak after withdrawal
5. Stop trading if DD hits 10%

### **Step 5: Recovery**
1. If stopped at 10% DD, pause 1-2 weeks
2. Review recent trades
3. Restart with 1% risk only
4. Gradually increase as DD reduces
