## 🎯 **STRATEGY 1: 10+ TRADES/MONTH**

### **"Bollinger Band Mean Reversion - Conservative"**

**Target:** 10+ trades/month  
**Tested Performance (6 months on USDJPY):**
- **Trades:** 39 total (6.5/month) 
- **Return:** -4.82% ⚠️
- **Win Rate:** 12.8%
- **Profit Factor:** 0.09

### **Strategy Rules:**

**Entry Rules:**
1. **Bollinger Bands:** 20 period, 2.5 standard deviations
2. **Long:** Price touches/breaks below lower BB, then closes back above it
3. **Short:** Price touches/breaks above upper BB, then closes back below it
4. **Session Filter:** Only trade during London (8-16 UTC) or NY (13-21 UTC) sessions

**Risk Management:**
- **Stop Loss:** 2.0 × ATR from entry
- **Take Profit 1:** BB middle band (scale out 50%)
- **Take Profit 2:** Entry + (2.0 × ATR) (scale out 25%)
- **Take Profit 3:** Opposite BB (scale out 25%)

**Position Sizing:**
- Risk 2% of capital per trade
- Max 1 trade open at a time

### **Why This Strategy?**
- **Mean reversion works in ranging forex markets**
- **Wide stops (2.0 ATR) avoid noise**
- **BB 2.5 std provides selective entries**
- **Targets middle reversion (realistic)**

### **Forex Pairs:**
- **Primary:** USDJPY (least loss: -4.82%)
- **Secondary:** EURUSD (-7.44% with 11.3 trades/mo)
- **Avoid:** GBPUSD (higher losses)
